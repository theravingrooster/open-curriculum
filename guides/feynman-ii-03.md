## Thesis

The flux through a closed surface equals the integral of the divergence; the circulation equals the integral of the curl.

## The main ideas

- The flux through a closed surface equals the integral of the divergence; the circulation equals the integral of the curl.

## The argument

Feynman states the idea, shows the mechanism that makes it hold, and works a concrete case. The same rule then applies to other situations of the same type.

Implication: apply the rule to a new problem rather than memorizing the example.
