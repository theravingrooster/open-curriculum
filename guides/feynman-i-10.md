## Thesis

Momentum is conserved when the forces between bodies are equal and opposite.

## The main ideas

- The third law implies conservation of momentum.
- The center of mass moves as if all the mass were concentrated there and all the external forces acted there.
- The chapter uses collisions as the example.

## Momentum

Feynman derives the conservation of momentum from the third law. The mechanism is that the internal forces cancel in pairs, so the total momentum changes only under external forces. The implication is that in an isolated system the total momentum is constant.
