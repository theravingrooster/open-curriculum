## Thesis

Physics is the study of the rules of the game, and the rules are few.

## The main ideas

- The basic laws are simple; the complexity of the world comes from the combinations of the simple parts.
- There are only a few kinds of particles and a few kinds of forces.
- The laws are not yet complete; we do not know all the rules.
- The method is to guess the rules and test them by experiment.

## Basic physics

Feynman lists the things that are known and the things that are not. The known rules cover the behavior of atoms, the forces between them, and the conservation laws. The unknown rules include the nature of the forces at very short distances and the origin of the masses of the particles.

The mechanism is that a small set of simple rules, applied to a large number of particles, produces the variety of the observed world. The implication is that understanding the rules is more powerful than cataloguing the phenomena.
