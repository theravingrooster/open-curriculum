## Thesis

Work is the integral of force along a path; potential energy is the negative of the work done by a conservative force.

## The main ideas

- The work-energy theorem relates the work done to the change in kinetic energy.
- For conservative forces, the work is independent of path and can be expressed as a potential.
- The chapter develops the concept of potential energy.

## Work and potential energy

Feynman defines work and shows that for certain forces the work depends only on the endpoints. The mechanism is that the force is the negative gradient of a potential function. The implication is that energy accounting becomes simpler when a potential exists.
