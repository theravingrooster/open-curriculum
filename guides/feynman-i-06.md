## Thesis

Probability is the language for describing incomplete knowledge of a system.

## The main ideas

- Probability is assigned to events according to the information available.
- The rules of probability follow from the requirement that the assignments be consistent.
- The chapter introduces the basic rules used later for quantum mechanics and statistical mechanics.

## Probability

Feynman derives the rules of probability from the requirement that the assignments remain consistent when information is added. The mechanism is that the probability of A or B is the sum when the events are mutually exclusive, and the probability of A and B is the product when the events are independent. The implication is that these rules apply to any situation in which knowledge is incomplete.
