## Thesis

The force of gravity is proportional to the product of the masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance.

## The main ideas

- Newton’s law of gravitation accounts for the motion of the planets and the fall of apples.
- The constant G is very small; gravity is weak compared with the other forces.
- The law is approximate; it is replaced by general relativity at high precision or strong fields.

## Gravitation

Feynman states the law and shows that it follows from the observed motions. The mechanism is that the force is central and inverse-square, which produces elliptical orbits with the sun at one focus. The implication is that the same law governs both celestial and terrestrial motions.
