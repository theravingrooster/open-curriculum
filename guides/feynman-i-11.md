## Thesis

Vectors are the language for quantities that have both magnitude and direction.

## The main ideas

- Position, velocity, acceleration, and force are vectors.
- The chapter establishes the algebra of vectors used throughout the lectures.
- Components in a coordinate system are the practical way to calculate.

## Vectors

Feynman introduces the vector as an object that can be added and multiplied by a scalar. The mechanism is that the rules of vector algebra follow from the geometric properties of directed segments. The implication is that the laws of physics can be written in a form that is independent of the choice of coordinates.
