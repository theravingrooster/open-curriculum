## Thesis

Force equals the rate of change of momentum; the second law plus a force law determines the motion.

## The main ideas

- Newton’s second law relates force to the rate of change of momentum.
- Combined with a force law, it determines the trajectory.
- The chapter works several examples of the law in action.

## Newton’s laws

Feynman states the second law and shows how it is used with a given force to find the motion. The mechanism is that the force determines the acceleration, and the acceleration determines the change in velocity and position. The implication is that the future motion is fixed once the initial conditions and the forces are known.
