## Thesis

The same physical laws govern a glass of wine and the stars.

## The main ideas

- Physics is connected to chemistry, biology, geology, and astronomy through the same underlying laws.
- A glass of wine is a complex system of atoms following simple rules.
- The connections are not yet fully worked out in every field.
- The method of physics is to look for the simple rules that apply across domains.

## Relation to other sciences

Feynman uses a glass of wine as the example. The wine is water, alcohol, and other molecules, all made of atoms. The atoms follow the same laws that govern the motion of the planets and the structure of crystals. The taste, the color, and the effect on the body are consequences of those laws, even if the calculation from first principles is not yet practical.

The implication is that the boundaries between the sciences are artificial; the same rules apply throughout.
