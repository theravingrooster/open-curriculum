## Thesis

The conclusion of the work-energy development shows how potential energy is used in practice.

## The main ideas

- Potential energy is a bookkeeping device for conservative forces.
- The total energy is conserved when only conservative forces act.
- The chapter finishes the development begun in the previous lecture.

## Work and potential energy (conclusion)

Feynman completes the argument. The mechanism is that the change in kinetic energy equals the work done by all forces, and the work of the conservative forces can be written as the decrease in potential. The implication is that the sum of kinetic and potential energy is constant for an isolated system with conservative forces.
