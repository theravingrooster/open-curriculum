## Thesis

Energy is conserved; it is a bookkeeping rule that holds in every process.

## The main ideas

- Energy is conserved; it can change form but the total does not change.
- The forms include kinetic, potential, heat, chemical, and nuclear.
- The conservation law is a consequence of the laws of motion and the forces.
- The rule is useful because it restricts the possible outcomes of a process.

## Conservation of energy

Feynman shows that the conservation of energy follows from Newton’s laws and the form of the forces. He then lists the different forms of energy and shows how they convert into one another. The mechanism is that the work done by the forces equals the change in kinetic energy, and the forces can be derived from a potential.

Implication: when analyzing a process, account for all forms of energy; if the books do not balance, a form has been omitted.
