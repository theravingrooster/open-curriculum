## Thesis

Motion is described by position as a function of time.

## The main ideas

- Velocity is the rate of change of position.
- Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.
- The chapter establishes the language of kinematics used in the rest of the volume.

## Motion

Feynman defines the derivatives that appear in the laws of dynamics. The mechanism is that the position, velocity, and acceleration are related by successive differentiation. The implication is that once the acceleration is known as a function of position and velocity, the motion can be determined.
