## Thesis

A molecular ratchet cannot extract work from a single heat bath; the second law holds statistically.

## The argument

Feynman develops the idea by starting from a concrete situation and extracting the general rule. The mechanism is stated directly, then illustrated with a specific case or calculation. The implication is that the same rule applies beyond the example.
