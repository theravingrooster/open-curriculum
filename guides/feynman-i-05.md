## Thesis

Time and distance are measured by comparing motions.

## The main ideas

- Time is measured by counting regular motions.
- Distance is measured by comparing lengths.
- The units are conventional; the physics is in the relations.
- The chapter establishes the practical definitions used in the rest of the lectures.

## Time and distance

Feynman discusses how clocks and rulers are used to define the quantities that appear in the laws. The mechanism is that any sufficiently regular process can serve as a clock, and any sufficiently rigid object can serve as a ruler. The implication is that the laws are independent of the particular choice of units.
