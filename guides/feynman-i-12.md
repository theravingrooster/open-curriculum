## Thesis

Forces have characteristics that determine the motion they produce.

## The main ideas

- The force law is what is given; the motion follows from the second law.
- Examples include gravity, springs, and friction.
- The chapter classifies the common force laws.

## Forces

Feynman discusses the properties of different forces. The mechanism is that each force law specifies how the force depends on position, velocity, or other quantities. The implication is that once the force law is known, the motion can be calculated.
