Mission

To make the best insights from the best books free and accessible to everyone.

Degrees certify time spent in a place. They don't certify understanding, and understanding is available elsewhere for almost nothing: nearly every idea worth learning was first written down by the person who worked it out.

The obstacle is time. A serious reading list takes years, and most books spend three hundred pages on thirty pages of ideas. The Open Curriculum does the extraction. Each guide states the book's argument, the ideas that change decisions, the evidence behind them, and where they fail.

A great book compresses decades of someone's work into a few hundred pages. A guide compresses the book into minutes. Nothing stays unless it carries an idea: no filler, no padding, nothing that wastes your time. What's left is thousands of years of accumulated expertise, available in a fraction of the time it took to create, so you can start where the best thinkers left off and go further.

The list is short and ordered. Books are chosen for how much they change the way you think, not for how popular they are. Every guide is held to one standard: each sentence has to carry information.

Free. No account, no ads, no certificate. The knowledge is the credential.
